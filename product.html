OutlineSave

A Chrome extension for navigating long ChatGPT and Gemini conversations, bookmarking key Q&As, and exporting or backing up chats.

  • Clickable outline navigation for long ChatGPT and Gemini chats
  • Jump directly to the section you need
  • Export conversations as Markdown or HTML, including HTML with a table of contents
  • Back up conversations directly to your own Google Drive
  • Local-first behavior for privacy and speed

ChatGPT and Gemini page content is processed locally in your browser. Google Drive backup runs only when you choose it and sends the selected export directly to your own Drive.
