Technology & Privacy Notes

Inbox is an optional local macOS app for Apple Silicon Macs. It can be downloaded separately from wisteriasoftware.uk and is not required for basic Wisteria Jump navigation.

  • Inbox data is stored on your device by default
  • AI summaries are triggered manually, not automatically
  • You provide your own API key inside Inbox when using summaries
  • The local-first setup keeps your data under your control
