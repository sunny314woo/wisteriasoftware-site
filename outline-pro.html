What It Does

ChatGPT Outline Pro automatically turns long ChatGPT sessions into a clickable table of contents. Whether you are writing, coding, studying, or running multi-round discussions, you can jump to any key point instantly instead of endless scrolling.

You can also highlight the specific Q&A turns worth keeping, then export either the full conversation or only the highlighted parts to local Markdown or Inbox.

ChatGPT page content is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers.

1. One-click Outline

FAQ

What is this product for?

It converts long ChatGPT conversations into a clickable outline so you can quickly find and review key points.

How do I activate after purchase?

You will receive an activation code by email (for example: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Enter it in the extension to activate. During the license validity period, you can re-activate again if needed.

What if WeChat Pay succeeds but the page looks stuck?

WeChat Pay can complete in asynchronous mode, so the success page may take a little longer to update. Please allow about 15 minutes for the payment status to finish syncing. The activation code email can also be delayed, so if you have not received it after 20 minutes, contact support@wisteriasoftware.uk.

Do you offer a trial?

Yes. New users get a 7-day Pro trial. During the trial, Pro limits apply to outline navigation, highlights, and exports.

Do I need to stay online after activation?

No. Internet is required for first activation only. After activation it can run locally offline.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free lets you click the first 15 outline items, add up to 2 highlights per conversation, and export up to 3 conversations per month. Pro and active trial remove those limits.

Can I export only the highlighted parts?

Yes. If you mark important Q&As first, you can export only those highlighted sections to local Markdown or Inbox instead of exporting the whole conversation.

Which environment is supported?

ChatGPT web usage scenarios in Chrome/Chromium browser extension environments.
