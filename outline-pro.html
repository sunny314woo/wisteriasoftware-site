Frequently asked questions

Features & trial

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free includes unlimited outline navigation, basic bookmarks, highlights, and notes, with limited outline search and one advanced HTML export per week. Pro unlocks unlimited outline search and searchable HTML exports, export filters, offline review, and priority support.

How does the 7-day trial start?

Free users start a 7-day Pro trial automatically the first time they click Advanced HTML export in the extension. No card is required, and the trial does not automatically become a paid plan.

How much free outline search is included?

Free users get a limited allowance. The exact cap has not been finalized; we will announce it in the extension and on this page. Pro outline search is unlimited.

Is outline navigation limited too?

No. Browsing and clicking the outline has no usage limit. The Free allowance applies to outline search, not ordinary outline navigation.

Can I filter and export bookmarked questions and answers?

Yes. Add level-one bookmarks to user questions and level-two bookmarks to AI answers. Filter bookmarks to show or export only the Q&As you want, in Markdown, HTML, or Inbox.

Purchase & license

Do 1-Year and Lifetime have different features?

They include the same Pro features. A 1-Year license is valid for one year with manual renewal; Lifetime requires no renewal. Both include unlimited outline search and HTML exports, search and filters in exported pages, preserved highlights, notes, and bookmarks, and priority email support.

What does “Lifetime License” mean?

A Lifetime License is a one-time purchase with no subscription or automatic renewal. Your license remains valid for future compatible versions of OutlineSave for as long as the product continues to be maintained and offered. You won’t need to purchase another license for compatibility updates.

Lifetime includes everything in 1-Year Pro and all future Pro updates, without renewal reminders. Unlimited searchable HTML exports remain available throughout the lifetime license term.

Is this a subscription?

No. Both Pro plans are one-time payments. The 1-Year plan expires after a year; Lifetime does not require renewal.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Purchases are covered by a 14-day refund policy. See the refund policy for details.

Payment & activation

How do I activate after purchase?

You receive an activation code by email. Enter it in the extension to activate Pro access.

The payment success page also shows your short activation code when payment is confirmed. Copy it into the extension’s activation field and follow the validation result shown there.

What if the activation email has not arrived?

Check the email address used at checkout and your spam folder. Once your code is ready, you can copy it or resend the email from the payment success page. That page shows the resend availability and cooldown. If it is still missing, contact support@wisteriasoftware.uk.

What if WeChat Pay looks stuck?

WeChat Pay can complete asynchronously. Please allow about 15 minutes for payment sync and about 20 minutes for the activation email before contacting support.

Usage & support

Which platforms and browsers are supported?

OutlineSave supports ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, and Gemini, with ChatGPT prioritized for compatibility updates. It works in Chrome and Chromium-based browsers that support Chrome extensions.

Do I need a separate account?

No separate OutlineSave account is required. The extension works locally with the content of the current supported AI chat page. If you are not logged in to ChatGPT, its own page display or loading restrictions may affect some extension features.

Does it work in Incognito mode?

Yes, if you allow it in Chrome. Open chrome://extensions, select OutlineSave’s Details, and enable “Allow in Incognito”. Chrome disables extensions in Incognito by default.

Does OutlineSave upload my chats?

No. Content from supported AI chat pages is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to Wisteria servers.

What if a ChatGPT update temporarily breaks OutlineSave?

Because OutlineSave works with the ChatGPT web interface, major interface changes may occasionally cause temporary compatibility issues.

OutlineSave is actively maintained, and I usually address compatibility issues within about two business days. More complex changes may take longer, and the release of an update may also depend on Chrome Web Store review times.

How can I get help?

Visit Help & Guides or email support@wisteriasoftware.uk. You can also contact us at info@wisteriasoftware.uk.
