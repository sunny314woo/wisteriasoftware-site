What It Does

Outline automatically turns long ChatGPT and Gemini sessions into a clickable table of contents. Whether you are writing, coding, studying, or running multi-round discussions, you can jump to any key point instantly instead of endless scrolling.

You can also bookmark the specific Q&A turns worth keeping, then export either the full conversation or only the bookmarked parts to Markdown, HTML, or Inbox. HTML exports can include a table of contents, and Google Drive backup saves files directly to your own Drive.

ChatGPT and Gemini page content is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers. When you explicitly choose Google Drive backup, the selected export is sent directly to your own Google Drive.
