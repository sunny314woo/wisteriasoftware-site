Understand search and navigation →

Free can search all outline entries. The extension tries its existing quick location paths, including ChatGPT search when available; these may not always reach the result. “Continue with outline navigation” follows the same allowance as clicking the directory: the first 100 entries per chat are free, with support for loaded content. Pro and an active trial unlock all directory navigation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel and keep using Pro?

Yes. Open your account card in the extension and choose Manage subscription to access Paddle billing. Cancel renewal there; Pro stays active until the paid period ends.

How do I activate after payment?

After payment is confirmed and your activation code is ready, it appears on the success page and is emailed to your checkout address. Enter it in OutlineSave. Subscription renewals extend the same code; you do not need to enter a new one each year. Previously purchased one-year and lifetime licenses keep their original terms.
