What It Does

Outline automatically turns long ChatGPT and Gemini sessions into a clickable table of contents. Whether you are writing, coding, studying, or running multi-round discussions, you can jump to any key point instantly instead of endless scrolling.

You can also bookmark the specific Q&A turns worth keeping, then export either the full conversation or only the bookmarked parts to Markdown, HTML, or Inbox. HTML exports can include a table of contents, and Google Drive backup saves files directly to your own Drive.

ChatGPT and Gemini page content is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers. When you explicitly choose Google Drive backup, the selected export is sent directly to your own Google Drive.

FAQ

What is this product for?

It converts long ChatGPT and Gemini conversations into a clickable outline so you can quickly find and review key points without a navigation usage limit.

Does Outline support Gemini?

Yes. Outline supports Gemini conversations in Chrome and Chromium-based browser extension environments.

Is outline navigation limited?

Unlimited. Outline navigation has no usage limit for ChatGPT or Gemini.

How do I activate after purchase?

You will receive an activation code by email (for example: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Enter it in the extension to activate. During the license validity period, you can re-activate again if needed.

What if WeChat Pay succeeds but the page looks stuck?

WeChat Pay can complete in asynchronous mode, so the success page may take a little longer to update. Please allow about 15 minutes for the payment status to finish syncing. The activation code email can also be delayed, so if you have not received it after 20 minutes, contact support@wisteriasoftware.uk.

Do you offer a trial?

Yes. New users get a 7-day Pro trial with Pro search, filters, bookmarks, and export access.

Do I need to stay online after activation?

No. Internet is required for first activation only. After activation it can run locally offline.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free helps you save, back up, and record AI chats. Pro 1-Year adds unlimited HTML knowledge pages, offline search, highlight and notes filters, faster knowledge retrieval, and priority email support.

What is the Lifetime plan?

Lifetime is a one-time purchase for long-term Pro access. It includes no subscription, no hidden renewals, all future updates, and support for independent development.

Does Outline upload my ChatGPT or Gemini content?

No. ChatGPT and Gemini page content is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers.

Can I export only the bookmarked parts?

Yes. If you bookmark important Q&As first, you can export only those saved sections to Markdown, HTML, or Inbox instead of exporting the whole conversation.

Which environment is supported?

ChatGPT and Gemini web usage scenarios in Chrome/Chromium browser extension environments.

Do I need a ChatGPT login or a separate user account to use this extension?

No. This extension does not require any separate user account system.

It works locally in your browser based on the content of the current ChatGPT or Gemini page. Your chats are processed on your device, and the extension does not require a separate login to function.

Does the extension work in Incognito mode?

Yes, it can work in Incognito mode.

However, Chrome disables extensions in Incognito mode by default. You need to manually allow the extension to run in Incognito mode from Chrome's extension settings.

You can enable it here: chrome://extensions → Details → Allow in Incognito

This is a Chrome browser setting, not an issue with the extension itself.

Why do some features not work when I am not logged in to ChatGPT?

If you are not logged in to ChatGPT, some features may be limited because of ChatGPT's own web restrictions.

Since the extension works based on the current page content, any limitation in how ChatGPT displays or loads the page may also affect how the extension behaves in certain cases.

This is not caused by the extension itself.
