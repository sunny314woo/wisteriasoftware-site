Frequently asked questions

What is included in Free and Pro?

The Free plan includes the complete two-level conversation outline and navigation, plus basic bookmarks, highlights, and notes. Pro adds advanced searchable HTML exports, export filters, offline review, and priority support. Free includes one advanced HTML export each week; Pro exports are unlimited. Directory search is currently available in public beta. It will become a core Pro feature, with a limited number of searches retained for Free users.

Do I need Pro for the full two-level outline and navigation?

No. The complete two-level outline and click-through navigation are included in Free. You do not need Pro to browse the outline or jump to a question or answer.

How do I start the 7-day free trial?

Click Advanced HTML export in the extension for the first time to start the 7-day Pro trial. No payment card is required. When the trial ends, the account returns to Free; it does not convert to a paid plan.

Can I save only the questions and answers that matter?

Yes. Add a first-level bookmark to a user question and a second-level bookmark to an AI answer. You can filter by bookmarks to show or export only the Q&As you want to keep.

What is the difference between the 1-Year and Lifetime plans?

Both plans include the same Pro features. The 1-Year license is valid for one year and requires manual renewal to continue. The Lifetime license has no expiry. The license period and entitlements are governed by the current purchase and license terms.

Is this a subscription? Will it renew automatically?

No. Both plans are one-time purchases and do not renew automatically. The 1-Year license expires after one year; you can choose to renew it manually.

Can I request a refund after purchase?

Purchases are covered by a 14-day refund policy. See the refund policy for eligibility and instructions.

How do I activate Pro after payment?

After payment is confirmed and the activation code is ready, it appears on the payment success page and is sent to the checkout email. Copy the code into OutlineSave and follow the activation status shown in the extension.

Why have I not received my activation code after WeChat Pay?

Some WeChat Pay orders take longer to sync payment confirmation or activation details. This usually takes around 15 minutes, though some orders may take longer and the email may arrive later. If you have been charged, do not pay again. Refresh the payment success page to check for your code; once it is ready, copy it there or use “Resend activation email,” and check the checkout inbox and spam folder. If it is still unresolved 30 minutes after payment, contact support@wisteriasoftware.uk with your order number or proof of payment.

What if I have not received the activation email?

Check the email address used at checkout and your spam folder. When the code is ready, you can copy it from the payment success page or resend the email if the button is available. If it is still missing, contact support@wisteriasoftware.uk.

Does OutlineSave upload my AI conversations?

AI conversation content is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to Wisteria servers. Google Drive backup is optional: you connect your own Google account and start it manually. Only the Markdown content you select is uploaded directly to your own Drive.

Which AI platforms and browsers are supported?

OutlineSave supports ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, and Gemini. It works in Chrome and in Edge versions compatible with Chrome extensions.

What should I do if a ChatGPT update causes an issue?

OutlineSave works with the ChatGPT web interface, so a site change can temporarily affect compatibility. Check Help & Guides and Troubleshooting for current steps. If the issue continues, contact support@wisteriasoftware.uk with your browser version, extension version, and a description of the problem.
