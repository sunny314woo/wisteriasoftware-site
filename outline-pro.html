What It Does

ChatGPT Outline Pro automatically turns long ChatGPT sessions into a clickable table of contents. Whether you are writing, coding, studying, or running multi-round discussions, you can jump to any key point instantly instead of endless scrolling.

ChatGPT page content is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers.

Feature Demo

1. One-click Outline

Click Outline in ChatGPT to auto-extract the current conversation question list.

2. Click to Jump

Click any item to smoothly scroll to the target message and highlight it.

3. Auto Refresh

When new messages appear or you switch conversations, the outline updates automatically.

4. Image-aware Marking

Image-only or mixed text-image prompts are labeled for faster review.

5. Flexible Sidebar

Switch left/right, resize by dragging, and toggle with Alt/Option + ←/→.

6. Markdown Export & Inbox Import

Download the current conversation as Markdown, or import it into Inbox for local review with preserved navigation.
