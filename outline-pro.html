What It Does

Wisteria Jump automatically turns long ChatGPT and Gemini sessions into a clickable table of contents. Whether you are writing, coding, studying, or running multi-round discussions, you can jump to any key point instantly instead of endless scrolling.

You can also highlight the specific Q&A turns worth keeping, then export either the full conversation or only the highlighted parts to local Markdown or Inbox.

ChatGPT and Gemini page content is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers.

Features

2. Click to Jump
