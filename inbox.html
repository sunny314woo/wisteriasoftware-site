Why Inbox Exists

AI is great at generating answers. It is much worse at helping you keep them.

Long conversations bury valuable work

Useful answers, prompts, and ideas get lost inside scroll-heavy sessions.

Good material gets fragmented

Screenshots, notes, references, and exported chats end up in too many places.

Reuse is harder than it should be

You end up re-asking questions instead of building on what you already learned.

Platform ownership is not your ownership

Too much of your work remains trapped inside tools that were never designed to be your long-term library.

What Inbox Is

Inbox is a personal knowledge workflow for people who use AI seriously. It helps you turn conversations, screenshots, and notes into material you can keep, review, and reuse over time.

Today, Inbox is available as Inbox Local for macOS. It is designed to work with Inbox Capture, a browser companion that makes saving useful content fast.

What You Can Do With Inbox

Capture what matters

Save useful conversations, screenshots, excerpts, and ideas before they disappear.

Organize it into something usable

Review saved material in a cleaner structure, with less scrolling and more signal.

Revisit and reuse

Return to past answers quickly, avoid repeated prompting, and build reusable knowledge.

Keep ownership

Use a local-first workflow so the material you save stays on your device and under your control.

Capture -> Organize -> Reuse -> Own
