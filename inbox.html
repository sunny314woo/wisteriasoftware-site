What Inbox Does

Inbox is designed for the reality of working with AI: long threads, valuable fragments, and the constant need to find things later. It processes structured AI information and gives you tools to stay organized.

Import structured conversations

Import ChatGPT and Gemini conversations exported via Outline. The directory structure is preserved—click any outline item to jump directly to that part of the conversation. No other tool does this.

Filter by tags, time, and more

Tag your imported material. Filter by tags, date ranges, and other criteria to surface exactly what you need, when you need it.

Capture from anywhere

Use Inbox Capture, the browser companion plugin, to save web text, screenshots, images, and notes while browsing. Paste from the clipboard directly into Inbox.

Plugin extensions

Inbox supports plugins for extending capture capabilities: grab articles, images, and structured content from the web and bring them into your local knowledge workflow.

Revisit and reuse

Stop re-asking the same questions. Build a growing personal library of AI-assisted work that you can search, filter, and build upon over time.

Your data, your device

Everything stays local. No cloud storage, no data uploaded to our servers. Inbox is a native macOS app that runs entirely on your machine.

The Workflow

Chat with AI → Outline the conversation → Export to Inbox → Tag, filter, review → Reuse

Inbox turns AI from a one-shot Q&A tool into a long-term system for thinking and knowledge. The Outline browser extension generates the structure; Inbox stores it, preserves it, and lets you work with it.
