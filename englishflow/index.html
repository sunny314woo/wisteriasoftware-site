Questions

Before you start

What is included in the 7-day trial?

You can try the FluentSocial AI features with hosted AI after verifying your email. No credit card is required.

What is the difference between hosted AI and Lifetime BYOK?

Monthly and Annual include the FluentSocial AI hosted service and require no API setup. Lifetime BYOK is a one-time software purchase that uses your own DeepSeek API key and does not include hosted AI usage.

Does FluentSocial AI post automatically?

No. FluentSocial AI only creates editable suggestions. You always review the final content and choose whether to send it.

Where is my DeepSeek API key stored?

Your key is stored in browser-managed local storage and used only for requests you initiate through FluentSocial AI.

Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes. Monthly and Annual plans can be cancelled from FluentSocial AI settings. Your access remains available until the end of the current billing period.
