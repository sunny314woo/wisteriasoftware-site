The important details

Before you buy

How do monthly and annual plans renew?

Monthly is US$6/month and Annual is US$49/year. Both are recurring Paddle subscriptions; cancel from EnglishFlow settings and the current period remains available until its end.

What happens when the public allowance runs out?

Normal users should have enough for everyday work. Individual high-frequency users may hit service limits; wait for the next reset or use your own API key to supplement.

Does Lifetime include the public AI service?

No. Lifetime unlocks EnglishFlow for your own API key. Choose Annual if you want Wisteria's public AI allowance.

How do refunds affect access?

Approved full refunds and chargebacks remove the corresponding grant. Partial refunds are reviewed manually and do not automatically shorten access. See the refund policy.
